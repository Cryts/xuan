# 选项该告知什么：成文规范与可搬用的手法

> 调研于 2026-09-20。**这是三份选项预告调研里最可执行的一份** —— 前两份给的是"方向"，
> 这一份给的是**成文标准、判据、与六种具体手法**，全部有一手原文。
> 与另两份配合阅读：`选项预告-模糊与清晰.md`（开发者案例）、
> `透明度的反方立场.md`（反方）、`谜语人-中文玩家实证.md`（玩家侧）。

## 一、最有分量的一条：Choice of Games 把"选项信息不足"写进了评分规则，扣到 0 分

CoG 是年产量数十部的**平台**，这条标准适用于其全部作品与投稿。
《How We Judge a Good Game—Part 2》（2017-03-24，Rachel E. Towers），
"Balanced Choices" 一项占**总分 15%**：

> "When reading the choice, **a player should have at least a rough estimate of what
> the outcomes of the different options are going to be.** If the choice is testing stats,
> they should have an idea of what option tests which stats... For example,
> 'I put on a baseball cap' doesn't tell the player much, while
> 'I put on a baseball cap **because it looks good on me**', or
> 'I put on a baseball cap **because I can pull it down to hide my face**'
> indicate what kind of stat they may be raising."

**惩罚条款（说明这是硬性要求，不是建议）：**

> "A game which contains many choices with only two options, with unbalanced options,
> **or with options that routinely do not contain enough information, may receive an 0
> in Balanced Choices.**"

<https://www.choiceofgames.com/2017/03/how-we-judge-a-good-game-part-2/>

**它暗含的方法论值得单独记**：**不是给数值，而是在选项文本里加"意图从句"**
（"因为……所以我这么做"），让玩家自己推断这一手在测什么。
—— 这正是本项目 `previewFor` 的做法，而 CoG 用一条评分规则把它变成了硬标准。

## 二、"盲选"的机理，被讲得最透的一篇

《How to Write Intentional Choices》（2016-12-22，Becky Slitt）
<https://www.choiceofgames.com/2016/12/how-to-write-intentional-choices/>

**(a) 点名老式游戏书的盲选：**

> "A standard practice in old choose-a-path gamebooks was to offer the options
> 'Go right' or 'Go left' without any more information...
> **The reader would have to choose randomly. Then, they'd probably fall off a cliff.**"

**(b) 核心论证 —— 盲选的代价取决于「能不能反悔」：**

> "in a choose-a-path gamebook, **you can easily flip back to start over, or peek ahead
> to see what happens with each option. That's not possible in a Choice of Games game.**
> The player needs to live with the consequences of their choices, and **may feel very
> frustrated if they make a choice with one intention in mind only to discover that the
> actual results are different.**"

> **对本作的含义**：本作**没有存档读档、没有时光倒流**（斗法"出手不推进节点"是有限例外）。
> 所以在"能不能反悔"这一维上，**本作与 CoG 同一处境，而不是与游戏书同一处境**。
> 盲选在本作里比在能翻书的介质里**更贵**。

**(c) 必须信号化的三项（可直接当 checklist）：**

> "before a choice, you should try to signal:
> - **the potential story results for each option**
> - **which stats might be tested**, if the option leads to a stat test
> - **the relative difficulty of each option**, if some options are harder than others."

**(d) 两个高危场景（必须预告，其余可酌情）：**

> "It's especially important in a few particular circumstances. Specifically,
> **when the potential consequences of an option are negative; and when different
> options within the same choice have different difficulty levels.** In these cases,
> it's up to the author to make sure that **the player has all the information they
> need to make the choice that they really want to make.**"

## 三、最有用的一句判据（Emily Short）：两种不确定要分开

Peter Mawhorter 的 choice poetics，经 Emily Short 转述：

> "there's a difference between **uncertainty whether an attempt will succeed**
> ... and **uncertainty about what success means**"

<https://emshort.blog/2019/04/09/choice-poetics-peter-mawhorter/>

**这是"预告设计"的分水岭**：
- **能不能成** → 该透明（给档位、给方向）
- **成了意味着什么** → 可以留白（道德重量、身份变化、代价的意义）

> 本作例：**"这次闭关能否突破"属于前者，宜给档位；"突破之后你还是不是你"属于后者，宜留白。**

**同一篇给出术语**：

> "**'blind choice' (the infamous 'go left or go right' choice where the player has no
> framing information to establish the stakes of the choice)**"

配套词表：**false choice**（看着会分叉其实不分叉）· **dead-end option**（即死选项）·
**dilemma** vs **flavor choice** · **unchoice**（只有一个选项）。

> ⚠️ **用 `blind choice` 这个词，不要用 `affordance`**（后者在本项目里是物品功能标签）
> 也不要用 `fuzzy feedback`（没有检索到的设计者这么用）。

## 四、透明会不会杀死惊喜 —— 本次最好的答案

Emily Short 评述 McKee《故事》的"期望落差"：

> "The gap gets trickier with advancement moves...
> **If you give the player an option and the result is not at all what the player
> expected, that feels like a lack of agency. If you give an option and the result
> is predictable, you've lost McKee's gap.**"

她给的解法是 **"yes, but…" 结构**：

> "Often, what you need is a **'yes, but' reaction. The player's choice succeeds,
> but there is a side effect or a cost that propels the story forward.**"
> 另有 "no, but…"："your attempt failed but it's changed the world in some other way,
> so it doesn't feel like the author was simply ignoring the player's choice."

<https://emshort.blog/2019/02/05/story-robert-mckee/>

> **可直接搬用的结论：预告"代价与风险"，保留"转折与反转"。**
> 玩家事前知道的应是"我要付出什么"，而不是"剧情会怎么翻"。

## 五、Fallen London 的官方写作规范（一手，内部规范完整公开）

<https://www.failbettergames.com/news/fallen-london-writer-guidelines-part-iii>

> "**A branch should almost always be a clear in-character action.** It's great to put
> colour and jokes in there, but **make sure the title, the description or - at the bare
> minimum - the button text makes clear what the character will be doing if you click
> this option.**"

<https://www.failbettergames.com/news/fallen-london-writer-guidelines-part-ii>

> "**Use [advisory text in square brackets] to provide the player with emphasised,
> explicit guidance and warnings.**"

**同篇的克制原则（防信息过载，与"给足信息"不矛盾）：**

> "**Be cautious - a string of quality changes in a result makes each one less important.**"

## 六、六种可搬用的"部分透露"手法（全部有原文支撑）

| 手法 | 出处 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| **形容词档位代替数字** | Fallen London | "A chancy challenge for your Persuasive quality" —— 明确告知成功率，但不给百分比 |
| **灰显锁住的选项** | CoG `*selectable_if` | 官方意图原文："it will appear in grey... **This gives players a hint that if they play the game again, they might be able to choose that option**" |
| **选项内嵌意图从句** | CoG（Becky Slitt） | "because I can pull it down to hide my face" |
| **选项前叙述盘一遍风险与难度** | CoG《Mecha Ace》 | 在给出四个战斗选项前，正文先替玩家把每个选项的风险、收益与难度都盘一遍 |
| **方括号警告文本** | Failbetter | `[advisory text in square brackets]` |
| **直接隐藏选项** | CoG `*if` | 与灰显相对；用于"这个人物此刻根本想不到" |

> **灰显 vs 隐藏的选择**：CoG 两种原语都有，官方给灰显的理由是
> "让玩家知道还有别的路，只是这次没够着"。**本作剧本里未满足的条件走的是「差什么」提示，
> 那已经比灰显更进一步** —— 继续保留。

## 七、一条必须承认的结构性悖论

Emily Short 引读者来信：

> "there is a very real problem with that format –
> **any investigation is necessarily telegraphed by the options given to the player.**"
> <https://emshort.blog/2016/11/21/mailbag-mysteries-in-cyoa/>

**你列出的选项，就已经定义了玩家能想象的世界边界。**
对"奇遇 / 探索"类内容同理：**列选项这个动作本身就是剧透。**

> 本作的含义：奇遇系统设计时要**显式决定"哪些路是玩家能想到的"** ——
> 这不是排版问题，是内容问题。

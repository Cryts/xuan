# 透明度之争：反方立场（Failbetter / Emily Short）

> 调研于 2026-09-20。**这一份是我们此前缺失的反方证据。**
> 前两份（Slay the Spire / Into the Breach）都指向"给足信息"，
> 而**本作在类型上更接近 Failbetter（叙事选择与后果）而不是 Slay the Spire（构筑）**——
> 所以反方立场必须认真对待，不能只挑支持自己的一方。

## 一、最强的反方：一个以"选择与后果"著称的厂商，明确拒绝让后果可量化

Failbetter《Curses and Consequences》（Hannah Flynn，2026-04-15）
<https://www.failbettergames.com/news/curses-and-consequences>

> "Some kinds of magic are almost like an alternate physics – there are strict rules
> you can learn and apply to create highly predictable, even **quantifiable**, outcomes.
> But another kind of magic – perhaps one truer to the word – **resists being treated
> like another branch of science or engineering**, by being sensitive to moral,
> psychological and social context."
>
> "So the Covenant of Weeds, for example, **resists systematic testing of its own
> boundaries**. Someone attempting that is exactly the kind of circumstance that would
> provoke reprisals."
>
> "It's partly **the gamer's instinct to interrogate and test systems**, but also the
> questions people in our world must have been asking themselves down the years"

**关键点**：他们把"**玩家想拆解系统的本能**"当作**要对抗的东西**来设计。
一个以选择与后果立身的厂商，**主动让它的世界抵抗优化**。

## 二、同一个立场的一句话版本：疑虑比结果更重

Failbetter 引 W.H. Auden（2014-08-14）<https://www.failbettergames.com/news/wh-auden-on-choice-and-consequence>

> "My subjective experience of living is one of having continually to make a choice
> between given alternatives, and it is this **experience of doubt and temptation**
> which seems more important and memorable to me **then** [原文如此] the actions I take
> when I have made my choice."

**Failbetter 认同这个判断** —— 选择**之前的不确定**，比选择之后的结算更重要。
如果预告把不确定压没了，"疑虑"也就没了。

## 三、但同一个厂商内部也有正方（这一点很重要）

Emily Short《Dialogue Expressiveness in Mask of the Rose》（2023-11-22，Failbetter 出品）
<https://emshort.blog/2023/11/22/dialogue-expressiveness-in-mask-of-the-rose/>

> "My goal in designing this way is to **let the player plan ahead**, adopt specific
> approaches to other characters, and play them out, as opposed to being entirely
> reactive to whatever dialogue choices happen to pop up in a given scene."

她同时点出了**替代做法的代价**：

> "Many games put those personality-characterising options side by side in a dialogue menu,
> but that means that **if you're trying to play a consistently melancholy character,
> you're stuck with your dialogue choices pretty much pre-decided for you**."

**所以不是"Failbetter 反对透明"** —— 是**同一个厂商里，两条线各按自己的核心体验取舍**：
- 《Mandrake》：核心体验是"世界不可被工程化" → 拒绝量化
- 《Mask of the Rose》：核心体验是"我要成为什么样的人" → 让玩家**提前规划**

**这与我给本作的切分是同一条：按内容的核心体验分，不搞一刀切。**

## 四、对本作的含义（修正我此前的说法）

我此前给 `previewFor`（选项后果预告）找的依据**全是正方**（Slay the Spire 否掉模糊、Into the Breach 为清晰牺牲酷点子）。
**这一份补上了反方，结论要更窄一点**：

- **预告说的是"这一手大致会动到什么"，不是"系统会给你多少分"** —— 这条仍然成立
- **但"给足信息"不该无差别铺开**。《玄》里确实有一批内容的核心体验是
  **"这个世界的规则我摸不透"**（`m_outer_god` 界外低语 / `m_forbidden_knowledge` 禁卷 /
  `m_dual_world` 双界真伪 那一类）。**对这些，量化的预告会直接摧毁它们的卖点。**
- **判据**：这条内容的核心体验是「**决策**」还是「**探索**」？
  决策 → 给方向与量级（`previewFor` 的做法）；探索 → 只给氛围（留白）。
  *（与 `谜语人-中文玩家实证.md` 第五节的切分一致，那份是从玩家抱怨侧得出的。）*
